Packs from the MakeHuman community, as listed in the editor's checked-in catalogue. A pack installs into MPFB's data directory from its catalogued URL, and only if its download matches the catalogue's hash. It never replaces a file already installed. Its assets appear in the editor's pickers with their licences. CC0 is cleared for anything; CC-BY is fine to use, and every face built from it credits the author in its manifest. Thumbnails load from the community's site.
